Sketch 001 · Hub Card Chip Row

Variant A — Pill · wrap-as-unit · muted origin

Full-pill chips (reuses today's .hub-card__badge geometry exactly — path of least resistance). Origin goes fully muted. Row wraps; the filled exposure cluster stays glued together (margin-left:auto) and wraps as one unit to line 2 when the card is narrow. Watch the long-hostname card and the INTERNET+FULL ACCESS card wrap.

Variant B — Rounded-rect · dedicated exposure line · roomier  ★ WINNER

Chips are rounded-rect (7px) instead of full pills, with more padding and an 8px gap. The exposure cluster is forced onto its own line (right-aligned) so the quiet agent · origin pills always share one clean line — most predictable when a hostname is long or both badges coexist.

Variant C — Pill · single line, never wraps · color-coded origin

Full-pill chips on a nowrap row: the origin pill truncates with an ellipsis so everything (incl. the exposure cluster) stays on one line — most compact, most stable height. Origin keeps its color-coded text (green local / blue remote) as colorblind reinforcement. Hover a truncated hostname pill for the full name.
