<objective>
Extend internal/relay/hub.go with the presence + typing layer: identity fields on
Subscriber, a reference-counted presenceRoster and a typingRoster of TTL timers on Hub,
and the broadcast/notify methods that fan presence and typing state to clients. This is
the engine for D-03 (per-person collapsed presence via refcount), D-04 (PersonKey is the
collapse key), and D-05 (typing carries the typer's alias). The 5s typing TTL via
time.AfterFunc is the only reliable mechanism that clears a typing indicator on abrupt
disconnect (PRESENCE-02).

Depends on Plan 01 for PresenceEntry/PresencePayload/TypingPayload, MakePresenceFrame,
and MakeTypingFrame.

Purpose: make the Hub's already-reference-counted subscriber set visible to clients as a
presence roster, and add volatile server-managed typing state.
Output: extended hub.go + hub_presence_test.go.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
