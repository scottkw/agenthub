| T-150-02 | Tampering | re-arm path in SetShellWebShareWarningEnabled | mitigate | Re-arm reset of shellWebShareWarned happens atomically inside one Lock + one saveSettingsToDisk; cannot be exploited to permanently suppress the warning (enabling always re-arms).
